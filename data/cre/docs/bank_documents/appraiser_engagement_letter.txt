Pacific Northwest Bank ("Bank") hereby engages Jones Lang LaSalle Americas, Inc. ("JLL")
to perform real estate appraisals on the following portfolio of commercial properties:

SCOPE OF ENGAGEMENT:

Total Properties: 180
- Office properties: 78 (including Cascadia Tower, Cedar Point, and other classified credits)
- Multifamily properties: 42
- Retail properties: 28
- Industrial properties: 18
- Hotel properties: 8
- Land: 6

TIMELINE:
- Phase 1 (classified credits, 35 properties): Complete by September 30, 2023
- Phase 2 (remaining office and hotel, 51 properties): Complete by November 30, 2023
- Phase 3 (multifamily, retail, industrial, land, 94 properties): Complete by January 31, 2024
- Total timeline: 180 properties within 180 days

APPRAISAL STANDARDS:
- All appraisals must comply with USPAP (Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice)
- Minimum two valuation approaches required (Income and Sales Comparison)
- For properties above $20M: All three approaches required (Income, Sales Comparison, Cost)
- Each appraisal subject to JLL internal review prior to delivery

FEE SCHEDULE:
- Properties under $10M appraisal value: $4,500 per appraisal
- Properties $10M - $50M: $7,500 per appraisal
- Properties above $50M: $12,500 per appraisal
- Estimated total engagement: $1,125,000

This engagement is required by the OCC as part of the Bank's asset quality review.
Results will be used for risk rating purposes, covenant testing, and ALLL calculations.
