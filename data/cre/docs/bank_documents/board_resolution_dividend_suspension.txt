PACIFIC NORTHWEST BANK
BOARD OF DIRECTORS

RESOLUTION NO. 2023-068
SUSPENSION OF COMMON STOCK DIVIDEND

WHEREAS, the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency ("OCC") has issued Consent Order
No. 2023-CE-0847 dated November 15, 2023, requiring, among other things, that the Bank
suspend common stock dividends until capital ratios are restored above well-capitalized
thresholds and maintained for two consecutive quarters; and

WHEREAS, the Bank's CET1 capital ratio has declined from 11.4% at year-end 2021 to
7.9% at year-end 2023, below the Board's internal target of 10.0%; and

WHEREAS, the Bank's CRE portfolio has experienced significant credit deterioration,
resulting in $87 million of charge-offs in 2023 and $95 million of additional provision
expense, both of which have reduced capital; and

WHEREAS, the preservation of capital is essential to maintaining the Bank's safety and
soundness during the current period of credit stress;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that:

1. The quarterly common stock dividend of $0.35 per share is hereby SUSPENDED effective
   immediately, beginning with the Q4 2023 dividend that would have been payable on
   January 15, 2024.

2. The dividend suspension shall remain in effect until BOTH of the following conditions
   are met for TWO CONSECUTIVE QUARTERS:
   (a) CET1 ratio is at or above 9.0% (above the 7.0% well-capitalized threshold plus
       a 200 basis point buffer); AND
   (b) Total Capital ratio is at or above 12.0%

3. Management shall provide the Board with a monthly capital report including projected
   timeline for dividend restoration.

4. The Board retains discretion to further extend the suspension period if credit conditions
   warrant, even if the quantitative thresholds above are met.

ADOPTED by unanimous vote of the Board of Directors at the meeting held on December 12, 2023.
