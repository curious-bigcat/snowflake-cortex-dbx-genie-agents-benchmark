CREDIT COMMITTEE MEMORANDUM

Date: February 8, 2021
Loan Number: CRE-2021-00847
Borrower: Cascadia Holdings LLC

Loan Officer: James Chen (ANL-0001), Senior Vice President, Portland Main Branch (BR-001)

RECOMMENDATION: APPROVE — $85,000,000 First Mortgage Loan

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

We recommend approval of an $85,000,000 first mortgage loan secured by Cascadia Tower,
a 42-story, 425,000 SF Class A office building in downtown Portland's Central Business District.
The borrower, Cascadia Holdings LLC, is a single-purpose entity managed by David Nakamura,
a seasoned CRE developer with 25+ years of Pacific Northwest market experience.

2. PROPERTY DESCRIPTION

Cascadia Tower is a LEED Gold-certified office tower completed in 2018. Key metrics:
- Total Rentable Area: 425,000 SF across 42 floors
- Current Occupancy: 94.2% (as of January 2021)
- Anchor Tenant: Apex Technologies Inc. (floors 15-28, 147,000 SF, lease expires 2028)
- Second Largest Tenant: Pacific Growth Capital (floors 30-35, 63,000 SF, lease expires 2026)
- Remaining Tenants: 12 tenants averaging 17,900 SF each
- Parking: 850 spaces (structured garage, levels B1-B3)
- Current Appraised Value: $131,000,000 (Jones Lang LaSalle, January 15, 2021)

3. LOAN TERMS

- Loan Amount: $85,000,000
- Loan-to-Value (LTV): 64.9% (based on $131M appraised value)
- Term: 7 years (maturity February 2028)
- Interest Rate: 3.875% fixed for 5 years, then ARM-5Y (SOFR + 215 bps)
- Amortization: 30-year schedule with balloon at maturity
- Monthly Payment (P&I): $399,421
- Prepayment: Yield maintenance for 3 years, then 1% penalty, open last 6 months

4. FINANCIAL ANALYSIS

Net Operating Income (2020 Actual): $9,215,000
Debt Service (Annual): $4,793,052
Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR): 1.92x (well above 1.35x policy minimum)
Breakeven Occupancy: 68.3%

5. COVENANTS

- Minimum DSCR: 1.35x (tested quarterly)
- Maximum LTV: 75% (tested annually at appraisal)
- Minimum Occupancy: 80% (tested quarterly)
- Minimum NOI: $6,375,000 annually
- Debt Yield minimum: 8.0%
- Interest Coverage Ratio minimum: 1.50x

6. RISK ASSESSMENT

Risk Rating: 2-PASS
Primary Risks:
- Tenant concentration: Apex Technologies represents 35% of rental income. Mitigant: 7-year
  remaining lease term provides stability through loan maturity.
- Market conditions: Portland CBD office vacancy at 11.8% (Q4 2020). Mitigant: Cascadia Tower
  is Class A with modern amenities; vacancy is concentrated in Class B/C.
- Interest rate risk: Fixed rate for initial 5 years mitigates near-term exposure.

Underwriting Exceptions: NONE

7. APPROVAL

Recommended by: James Chen, SVP, Commercial Real Estate
Approved by: Credit Committee (unanimous)
