5. CONCLUSION

Without the capital plan actions, PNB would breach well-capitalized thresholds under
severe adverse conditions. The capital plan provides approximately 170 bps of cushion,
restoring CET1 to 7.8% under stress. However, this leaves minimal buffer — any scenario
worse than severe adverse could still threaten capital adequacy.
