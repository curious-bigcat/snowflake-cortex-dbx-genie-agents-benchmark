1. OVERVIEW

This memorandum documents the Bank's ALLL methodology following the transition to the
Current Expected Credit Losses (CECL) standard (ASC 326). The methodology was updated in
Q4 2023 in response to OCC examination findings regarding inadequate loss estimates for
the CRE portfolio segment.

2. METHODOLOGY COMPONENTS

The Bank's ALLL is calculated as:

  ALLL = Quantitative Reserve + Qualitative Adjustment (Q-Factor) + Specific Reserve

a) QUANTITATIVE RESERVE (Historical Loss Rate Method)
   - Based on the Bank's 5-year average loss rate by loan segment
   - Adjusted for current economic conditions using a 2-year reasonable and supportable forecast
   - Reversion to long-term average over a 1-year reversion period

   Historical Loss Rates (5-year average):
   Segment         Loss Rate
   CRE Office      0.85%
   CRE Multifamily 0.32%
   CRE Retail      0.95%
   CRE Industrial  0.28%
   CRE Hotel       1.45%
   CRE Land        2.10%
   C&I             0.55%
   Residential     0.18%

b) QUALITATIVE ADJUSTMENTS (Q-Factors)
   The Bank applies Q-factor adjustments to capture risks not reflected in historical loss data.
   Q-factors are reviewed quarterly by the Credit Risk Committee.

   Q-FACTOR HISTORY (CRE OFFICE SEGMENT):
   Period     Q-Factor   Rationale
   Q1 2021    +0.50%     Baseline — pandemic uncertainty
   Q2 2021    +0.50%     No change
   Q3 2021    +0.50%     No change
   Q4 2021    +0.50%     No change
   Q1 2022    +0.75%     Rising interest rates, early vacancy signals
   Q2 2022    +1.00%     Tech sector cooling, Portland vacancy rising
   Q3 2022    +1.00%     No change (NOTE: OCC found this should have been higher)
   Q4 2022    +1.00%     No change
   Q1 2023    +1.50%     Apex Technologies announced Portland reduction
   Q2 2023    +2.00%     Multiple tenant departures, appraisal declines accelerating
   Q3 2023    +3.00%     Post-OCC exam adjustment
   Q4 2023    +3.50%     Full market deterioration recognition

   The OCC examination found that Q-factors in Q3 2022 through Q1 2023 were understated by
   approximately 1.0-1.5 percentage points, contributing to the $45-65M ALLL shortfall cited
   in the Consent Order.

c) SPECIFIC RESERVES
   Individual impairment analysis for loans rated 6-SUB or worse with balances exceeding $5M.
   Currently 78 loans with specific reserves totaling $142M.

4. OCC-IDENTIFIED SHORTFALL

The OCC estimates an ALLL shortfall of $45-65M, primarily in the CRE Office segment.
The Bank is addressing this through:
- Updated Q-factors (see Q4 2023 adjustment above)
- Expanded specific reserve analysis for CRE office loans rated 5-SS or worse
- Engagement of independent ALLL validation consultant (PricewaterhouseCoopers)
