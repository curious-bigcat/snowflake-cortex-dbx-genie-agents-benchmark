1. MARKET CONDITIONS

The Portland CBD office market has experienced significant deterioration since our 2021
valuation. Key market metrics:

- Portland CBD Office Vacancy Rate: 31.2% (Q3 2023), up from 11.8% (Q4 2020)
- Average Class A Asking Rent: $32.50/SF (Q3 2023), down from $41.75/SF (Q4 2020), a 22.1% decline
- Net Absorption (2023 YTD): Negative 1.8 million SF across Portland metro
- New Supply: 650,000 SF delivered in 2022-2023, further pressuring vacancy

The primary driver is the shift to hybrid and remote work models, accelerated by the
2023 Pacific Northwest tech sector layoffs. Major employers including Apex Technologies,
Intel, and Nike have all reduced their Portland office footprints.

2. PROPERTY-SPECIFIC CHANGES

Cascadia Tower occupancy has declined from 94.2% to 38.0%:

- Apex Technologies (anchor tenant): Exercised early termination clause in June 2023,
  vacating floors 15-28 (147,000 SF). Termination penalty of $4.2M was paid but does not
  offset the lost rental income of approximately $6.2M annually.
- Pacific Growth Capital: Reduced footprint from 63,000 SF to 31,500 SF (floors 32-33 only).
  Lease expires December 2026 with no renewal commitment.
- Three smaller tenants (combined 42,000 SF) vacated at lease expiry in Q1-Q2 2023.
- Current occupied SF: 161,500 SF (38.0% occupancy)

3. VALUATION METHODOLOGY

We employed three approaches:

a) Income Approach (Primary): $78,000,000
   - Current NOI: $4,100,000 (annualized from Q3 2023 actual)
   - Stabilized NOI (projected): $6,800,000 (assumes gradual lease-up to 65% over 3 years)
   - Cap Rate: 7.25% (reflecting current market risk premium for CBD office)
   - Discounted Cash Flow (10-year): $78,200,000

b) Sales Comparison: $74,000,000
   - Three comparable sales: Pioneer Courthouse Square ($185/SF, June 2023),
     Lloyd Center Tower ($165/SF, April 2023), Morrison Bridge Plaza ($178/SF, August 2023)
   - Adjusted average: $174/SF x 425,000 SF = $73,950,000

c) Cost Approach: $95,000,000
   - Replacement cost less depreciation (not relied upon due to market disconnect)

RECONCILED VALUE: $78,000,000 (weighted: 60% income, 30% sales, 10% cost)
