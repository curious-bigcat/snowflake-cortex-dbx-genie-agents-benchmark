6. OUTLOOK

The CRE office portfolio will continue to deteriorate through 2024 as tenant leases
expire without renewal. Portland CBD office vacancy is projected to reach 35-38%
by mid-2024. The Bank's workout pipeline of $2.1 billion in distressed CRE loans
will require 18-36 months to resolve.

The capital plan and CRE concentration reduction are critical to maintaining
adequate capitalization through the workout period.
