ENVIRONMENTAL SITE ASSESSMENT — PHASE I

Project: Cascadia Tower
Address: 1200 SW Main St, Portland, OR 97205
Client: Pacific Northwest Bank (Lender)
Date: January 10, 2021
Prepared by: Terracon Consultants, Inc.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Terracon has completed a Phase I Environmental Site Assessment (ESA) of the Cascadia Tower
property in accordance with ASTM Standard Practice E1527-13.

FINDINGS: NO RECOGNIZED ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS (RECs) IDENTIFIED

The assessment included:
1. Records review (federal, state, and local environmental databases)
2. Historical use review (Sanborn maps, aerial photographs, city directories)
3. Site reconnaissance (interior and exterior inspection)
4. Interviews with current owner and building management

Key observations:
- The property was developed in 2016-2018 on a previously commercial site (parking garage)
- No evidence of underground storage tanks (USTs), hazardous materials storage, or
  contamination from adjacent properties
- Building constructed with modern materials; no asbestos-containing materials identified
- No evidence of mold, radon, or other environmental hazards
- The property is NOT located in a flood zone (FEMA Zone X — minimal flood hazard)
- Earthquake risk: Portland is in Seismic Design Category D; building was designed to
  current seismic code requirements

OPINION: This property presents no recognized environmental conditions that would
materially affect its value or present a risk to the lender.

NOTE: This Phase I ESA was properly on file at the time of loan origination.
Per the Internal Audit Report (IA-2023-017), 15% of CRE originations above $5M during
2021-2022 were MISSING required Phase I reports. Cascadia Tower is NOT one of those
properties — this file is complete.
