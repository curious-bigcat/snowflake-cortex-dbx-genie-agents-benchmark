ITEM 1: OCC CONSENT ORDER 2023-CE-0847

Mr. Hartwell presented a summary of the Consent Order issued November 15, 2023.
Key requirements and proposed responses were discussed:

Capital Plan (90-day deadline: February 13, 2024):
- $175M subordinated debt issuance: CFO Warren reported that Goldman Sachs and
  JPMorgan have been engaged as placement agents. Indicative terms: 10-year maturity,
  7.5% coupon, expected closing Q1 2024.
- $400M performing CRE loan sale: Head of CRE Chen reported a pool of 187 performing
  loans has been identified. Blackstone Real Estate Debt Strategies has submitted an
  indicative bid of 92 cents on the dollar ($368M). Additional bids expected from
  Brookfield and Starwood.
- Risk-weighted asset reduction of $200M: CRO Park outlined a plan to reduce
  unfunded commitments and optimize risk weights through guarantee structures.

RESOLUTION: The Board unanimously approved management's capital plan framework
and authorized the CEO to finalize the subordinated debt offering and loan sale within
the parameters presented.

ITEM 2: DIVIDEND SUSPENSION

Ms. Warren presented the financial impact of dividend suspension as required by the
Consent Order. The Bank has paid $0.35/share quarterly ($14M/quarter) through Q2 2023.
Q3 dividend was already suspended pending the OCC examination conclusion.

RESOLUTION: The Board unanimously resolved to suspend all common stock dividends
effective immediately, until capital ratios are restored above "well-capitalized" thresholds
for two consecutive quarters, as required by Article I(b) of the Consent Order.

ITEM 3: CRE PORTFOLIO UPDATE

Mr. Chen presented the CRE portfolio health dashboard:
- Total CRE exposure: $8.7B (420% of capital)
- CRE Office: $3.85B (185% of capital) — primary area of concern
- Loans rated 5-SS or worse: 34% of CRE office portfolio (up from 6% at YE 2021)
- Workout pipeline: $2.1B in distressed loans under active workout
- Non-accrual CRE loans: $1.2B (14% of CRE portfolio)
- Charge-offs YTD: $87M (vs. $12M full year 2022)

Notable individual credits discussed:
- Cascadia Tower (CRE-2021-00847): Workout proposal approved for $16.5M principal
  reduction. Charge-off to be recorded in Q4 2023.
- Mercer Industrial Park (CRE-2020-01205): Borrower (Puget Industrial LLC) in default.
  Foreclosure proceedings initiated. Expected loss: $8-12M.
- Cedar Point Office Complex (CRE-2022-00456): Placed on non-accrual. DSCR 0.62x.
  Borrower has been unresponsive. Workout officer assigned.

ITEM 4: STRESS TEST RESULTS

Mr. Park presented the results of the internal stress test (severe adverse scenario):
- Assumptions: 18% additional office value decline, 45% vacancy, 6.5% unemployment
- Projected CET1 impact: Decline from current 8.1% to 6.1%
- This is BELOW the 7.0% "well-capitalized" threshold
- Additional provision expense under stress: $340M
- Capital plan actions (sub debt + loan sale) would restore CET1 to approximately 7.8% under stress

RESOLUTION: The Board directed management to include the stress test results in the
Capital Plan submission to the OCC and to implement monthly stress testing going forward.
