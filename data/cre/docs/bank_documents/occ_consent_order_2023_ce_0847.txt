UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY
OFFICE OF THE COMPTROLLER OF THE CURRENCY

In the Matter of:
Pacific Northwest Bank
Portland, Oregon

Consent Order No. 2023-CE-0847

The Comptroller of the Currency of the United States of America ("Comptroller"), through
the Examiner-in-Charge and his staff ("OCC"), has examined Pacific Northwest Bank ("Bank")
and has identified unsafe or unsound banking practices and violations of law relating to
the Bank's commercial real estate lending activities.

FINDINGS

The OCC finds that:

1. CRE CONCENTRATION

(a) As of September 30, 2023, the Bank's total CRE exposure was approximately $8.7 billion,
    representing 420% of the Bank's total risk-based capital. This significantly exceeds
    the 300% threshold established in the Interagency Guidance on Concentrations in
    Commercial Real Estate Lending (SR 07-1, December 2006).

(b) The Bank's CRE office sub-concentration was approximately 185% of capital, with no
    Board-approved sub-limit for this property type.

(c) Geographic concentration: 72% of CRE exposure is in the Portland-Seattle-Boise corridor,
    creating correlated risk to Pacific Northwest economic conditions.

2. UNDERWRITING DEFICIENCIES

(a) The OCC's examination found that 23% of CRE loans originated during calendar years
    2021 and 2022 contained one or more underwriting exceptions, including:
    - 847 loans with DSCR below the Bank's 1.25x policy minimum (representing $2.3B)
    - 312 loans with LTV above the Bank's 75% policy maximum (representing $1.1B)
    - 156 loans missing required Environmental Phase I Site Assessment reports
    - 47 loans approved without formal Credit Committee review as required for amounts
      exceeding $10 million

(b) Exception tracking and reporting to the Board was inadequate. The Bank's exception
    report understated the actual exception rate by approximately 40%.

3. ALLOWANCE FOR LOAN AND LEASE LOSSES (ALLL)

(a) The Bank's ALLL methodology does not adequately capture the deterioration in the
    CRE office portfolio. Qualitative adjustment factors (Q-factors) were last updated
    in Q2 2022 and do not reflect current market conditions.

(b) The Bank's total ALLL of $187 million (as of September 30, 2023) appears insufficient
    given the risk profile of the CRE portfolio. OCC examiners estimate an ALLL shortfall
    of approximately $45-65 million.

4. RISK RATING TIMELINESS

(a) The OCC's examination identified 234 CRE loans where risk rating downgrades were
    delayed by an average of 2.3 quarters from the point when financial deterioration
    signals were evident.

(b) As a result, 34% of the CRE office portfolio is now rated Special Mention (5-SS)
    or worse, up from 6% at year-end 2021.

5. CAPITAL ADEQUACY

(a) The Bank's CET1 capital ratio has declined from 11.4% at year-end 2021 to 8.1% as of
    September 30, 2023.

(b) Under the OCC's severe stress scenario, the Bank's CET1 ratio is projected to decline
    to approximately 6.1%, below the 7.0% "well-capitalized" threshold.

(c) The Bank continued to pay common stock dividends of $0.35/share quarterly through
    Q2 2023 (payout ratio of 62%), inconsistent with the Bank's risk profile.

6. EXAMINATION FINDINGS

(a) The OCC has identified 47 total examination findings, of which:
    - 12 are classified as Matters Requiring Immediate Attention (MRIA)
    - 22 are classified as Matters Requiring Attention (MRA)
    - 13 are classified as Recommendations (REC)

(b) Of the 12 MRIA findings, 8 were originally identified in the 2022 examination and
    remain unresolved.

CONSENT ORDER

NOW THEREFORE, the Bank, without admitting or denying the above findings, consents
to the following requirements:

ARTICLE I — CAPITAL PLAN (90-day deadline)

The Bank shall, within ninety (90) days of this Order, submit to the OCC a Capital Plan
that includes:
(a) Specific actions to maintain CET1 ratio above 8.0% under baseline and above 7.0%
    under stress scenarios, including but not limited to:
    - Issuance of subordinated debt (minimum $175 million)
    - Sale of performing CRE loans (target: $400 million)
    - Risk-weighted asset reduction through portfolio optimization (target: $200 million)
(b) Suspension of common stock dividends until capital ratios are restored to
    "well-capitalized" levels and maintained for two consecutive quarters
(c) Monthly capital reporting to the OCC

ARTICLE II — CRE CONCENTRATION MANAGEMENT (120-day deadline)

(a) Establish Board-approved concentration limits for total CRE and each CRE sub-type
(b) Develop a plan to reduce total CRE concentration below 350% of capital within 18 months
(c) Cease origination of new CRE office loans until concentration is below 150% of capital

ARTICLE III — ALLL METHODOLOGY (60-day deadline)

(a) Engage an independent third party to validate the ALLL methodology
(b) Update Q-factors to reflect current market conditions
(c) Establish quarterly Q-factor review process with Board oversight

ARTICLE IV — RISK RATING (30-day deadline)

(a) Complete re-rating of entire CRE portfolio within 30 days
(b) Implement monthly credit quality monitoring dashboard
(c) Establish maximum 30-day timeline for rating downgrades upon trigger events

ARTICLE V — UNDERWRITING (60-day deadline)

(a) Revise CRE underwriting standards to eliminate identified gaps
(b) Establish independent exception tracking and reporting to the Board
(c) Require Credit Committee approval for all CRE loans exceeding $5 million

ARTICLE VI — EXAMINATION FINDINGS (ongoing)

(a) Remediate all 12 MRIA findings within 180 days
(b) Remediate all 22 MRA findings within 365 days
(c) Provide monthly remediation progress reports to the OCC

ARTICLE VII — BOARD OVERSIGHT

(a) The Board shall review this Order at each regular meeting
(b) The Board shall appoint a Compliance Committee to oversee implementation
(c) The Chief Credit Officer shall provide monthly written reports to the Board

EFFECTIVE DATE: November 15, 2023
