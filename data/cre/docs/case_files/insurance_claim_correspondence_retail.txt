PACIFIC NORTHWEST BANK
CORRESPONDENCE FILE

Re: Insurance Claim — Willamette River Plaza
Property: 800 SW Naito Parkway, Portland, OR 97204
Loan Number: CRE-2021-01567
Borrower: Willamette Development Corp
Insurance Carrier: Hartford Fire Insurance Company
Claim Number: HFI-2023-44891

TIMELINE:

March 3, 2023 — INCIDENT
A magnitude 4.2 earthquake centered near Portland caused structural damage to
Willamette River Plaza, a 32,000 SF retail property. Damage includes:
- Foundation cracks in east wall (structural engineering report attached)
- HVAC ductwork separation on floors 2-3
- Facade tile displacement (cosmetic but affecting ADA compliance at entrance)
- Water damage from sprinkler line rupture in basement storage area

March 8, 2023 — CLAIM FILED
Borrower filed insurance claim with Hartford. Estimated damage: $2,800,000.
Bank loss payee endorsement on file; all proceeds to be applied to repairs.

April 12, 2023 — ADJUSTER REPORT
Hartford adjuster assessed damage at $2,100,000 (30% below borrower's estimate).
Disputed items: Foundation crack repair ($400K — adjuster considers preexisting),
facade tile replacement ($300K — adjuster classifies as cosmetic, not covered).

May 20, 2023 — BORROWER DISPUTE
Borrower retained independent structural engineer who confirmed earthquake as
proximate cause of foundation cracks. Bank's loss mitigation team supporting
borrower's position as it protects collateral value.

August 1, 2023 — SETTLEMENT
Hartford agreed to $2,450,000 settlement. Funds disbursed to repair escrow
account controlled by Bank. Repairs commenced September 2023.

IMPACT ON COLLATERAL:
Pre-earthquake appraised value: $15,200,000
During repair (reduced functionality): $12,800,000
Post-repair projected value: $14,500,000 ($700K permanent diminution due to
seismic risk repricing in Portland metro market)

Current Loan Balance: $13,800,000
LTV (post-repair): 95.2% (above 75% covenant)
Borrower has been notified of covenant breach; cure period applies.
