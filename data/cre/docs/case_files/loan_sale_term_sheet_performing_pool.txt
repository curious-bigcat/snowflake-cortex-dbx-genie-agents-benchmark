Implied Loss to Seller: $32,960,000 (8.0%)

3. KEY TERMS

- Closing: On or before March 15, 2024
- Due Diligence Period: 45 days from execution
- Deposit: 2% ($7,580,800) non-refundable after due diligence period
- Servicing: Seller retains servicing for 90 days post-closing, then transfers to Buyer's servicer
- Representations: Standard commercial loan sale reps (no recourse post-close)
- Breakage: If Buyer fails to close, deposit forfeited; if Seller fails, $15M liquidated damages

4. RATIONALE

This sale is part of PNB's Capital Plan submitted to the OCC under Consent Order 2023-CE-0847.
The sale will:
- Reduce CRE concentration by approximately $412M (from $8.7B to $8.3B)
- Reduce CRE-to-capital ratio from 420% to approximately 400%
- Release approximately $33M of risk-weighted assets (net of loss)
- Improve CET1 ratio by approximately 0.4% (net of loss recognition)

5. SELLER REPRESENTATIONS

Seller represents that:
- All loans in the pool are current (0 DPD) as of the cut-off date
- No loans are subject to pending or threatened litigation
- All collateral has been appraised within the past 24 months
- Environmental Phase I reports are on file for all loans with balance exceeding $5M
