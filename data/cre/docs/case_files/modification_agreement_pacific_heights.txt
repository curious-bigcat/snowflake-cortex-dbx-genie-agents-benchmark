LOAN MODIFICATION AGREEMENT

Date: October 25, 2023
Loan Number: CRE-2021-01089
Lender: Pacific Northwest Bank
Borrower: Pacific Heights Partners LP

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Lender made a loan to Borrower in the original principal amount of $28,000,000
(the "Loan") pursuant to a Loan Agreement dated April 10, 2021;

WHEREAS, the Loan is secured by a first-priority deed of trust on Pacific Heights Apartments,
a 180-unit multifamily property located at 1500 NW 23rd Ave, Portland, OR 97210;

WHEREAS, Borrower has requested modification of certain terms of the Loan due to
increased operating costs and softening rental market conditions;

MODIFICATION TERMS

1. INTEREST RATE: Reduced from 5.750% to 4.250% fixed for 24 months (effective
   November 1, 2023 through October 31, 2025), then reverting to 5.750%.

2. MATURITY DATE: Extended from April 10, 2028 to April 10, 2030 (2-year extension).

3. PRINCIPAL PAYDOWN: Borrower shall make a one-time principal payment of $2,000,000
   within 30 days of execution of this Agreement, reducing the outstanding balance
   from $26,200,000 to $24,200,000.

4. MODIFIED COVENANTS:
   - DSCR minimum: 1.15x (reduced from 1.30x) for 12 months, then 1.25x
   - LTV maximum: 80% (unchanged)
   - Occupancy minimum: 85% (reduced from 90%)

5. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS:
   - Cash sweep: 50% of excess cash flow applied to principal until balance reaches $22,000,000
   - Monthly reporting: Borrower to provide monthly rent roll and operating statement
   - No additional debt: Borrower may not incur additional secured debt on the property

PROPERTY STATUS:
Current Occupancy: 91.2%
Current NOI (annualized): $1,850,000
Current Appraised Value: $33,500,000
Current LTV: 72.2% (post-paydown)
Modified DSCR: 1.52x (at modified rate)
