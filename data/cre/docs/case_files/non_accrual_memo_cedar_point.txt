PACIFIC NORTHWEST BANK
CREDIT MEMORANDUM — NON-ACCRUAL RECOMMENDATION

Date: September 28, 2023
Loan Number: CRE-2022-00456
Borrower: Cedar Point Ventures LLC
Property: Cedar Point Office Complex, 2800 NE Sandy Blvd, Portland, OR 97232

RECOMMENDATION: Place on Non-Accrual Status

1. BACKGROUND

Cedar Point Office Complex is a 3-building, 85,000 SF suburban office campus.
The loan was originated January 18, 2022 for $19,000,000 at 5.25% fixed.
Current balance: $18,400,000.

2. DETERIORATION

The property has experienced rapid deterioration:
- Occupancy declined from 88% at origination to 42% (current)
- Two anchor tenants (combined 38,000 SF) departed in Q1 and Q2 2023
- Current NOI: $1,142,000 (annualized) vs. debt service of $1,235,000
- DSCR: 0.92x (below 1.00x — cash flow insufficient to service debt)
- Current appraisal (August 2023): $14,200,000 (LTV: 130%)

3. PAYMENT STATUS

The borrower has been 90+ days past due since July 2023. Three consecutive payments
missed. The borrower is unresponsive to communications.

4. NON-ACCRUAL CRITERIA

Per PNB Policy Section 12.4, a loan should be placed on non-accrual when:
(a) Principal or interest is past due 90 days or more; AND
(b) Full collection of principal and interest is doubtful

Both criteria are met.

5. ACCRUED INTEREST REVERSAL

Accrued and uncollected interest of $241,500 (90 days) to be reversed against
current period interest income.

6. SPECIFIC RESERVE

Recommended specific reserve: $4,200,000 (based on collateral shortfall)
Calculation: $18,400,000 (balance) - $14,200,000 (appraisal) = $4,200,000

Approved by: Robert J. Hartwell, Chief Credit Officer
