RISK FACTORS (selected)

COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE CONCENTRATION RISK

A significant portion of our loan portfolio consists of commercial real estate loans.
As of December 31, 2023, our CRE loans totaled $8.7 billion, representing approximately
55.8% of total loans and 420% of total risk-based capital. This concentration exceeds
the 300% threshold established in the 2006 Interagency Guidance on Concentrations in
Commercial Real Estate Lending (SR 07-1).

Our CRE office portfolio of $3.85 billion has been adversely affected by:
- The shift to hybrid and remote work reducing office space demand
- The 2023 Pacific Northwest technology sector contraction, which resulted in
  significant tenant departures across our Portland and Seattle CBD office properties
- Rising interest rates increasing borrower debt service costs
- Cap rate expansion reducing property values

As a result, 34% of our CRE office portfolio is rated Special Mention or worse, and
non-performing CRE loans increased from $57 million at year-end 2021 to $1.88 billion
at year-end 2023.

OCC CONSENT ORDER

On November 15, 2023, we entered into a Consent Order (No. 2023-CE-0847) with the
Office of the Comptroller of the Currency. The Consent Order requires us to, among
other things:
- Submit a Capital Plan to maintain adequate capital ratios
- Reduce CRE concentration below 350% of capital within 18 months
- Suspend common stock dividends
- Remediate all examination findings
- Cease new CRE office originations until concentrations are reduced

We are actively implementing the requirements of the Consent Order. We cannot assure
that our remediation efforts will be completed within the required timeframes or that
additional regulatory actions will not be taken.
