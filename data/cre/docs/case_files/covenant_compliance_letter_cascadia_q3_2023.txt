Dear Mr. Nakamura:

This letter constitutes formal notice that Pacific Northwest Bank ("Lender") has determined
that the following covenants under the Loan Agreement dated February 15, 2021 (the "Agreement")
are in breach as of the September 30, 2023 testing date:

1. DEBT SERVICE COVERAGE RATIO (Section 6.2(a))
   Required: Minimum 1.35x
   Actual: 0.87x
   Deficiency: 0.48x below minimum

2. LOAN-TO-VALUE RATIO (Section 6.2(b))
   Required: Maximum 75.0%
   Actual: 100.6%
   Deficiency: 25.6 percentage points above maximum

3. OCCUPANCY RATE (Section 6.2(c))
   Required: Minimum 80.0%
   Actual: 38.0%
   Deficiency: 42.0 percentage points below minimum

4. NET OPERATING INCOME (Section 6.2(d))
   Required: Minimum $6,375,000 annually
   Actual: $4,100,000 (annualized)
   Deficiency: $2,275,000 below minimum

Pursuant to Section 7.1 of the Agreement, Borrower has thirty (30) calendar days from
the date of this notice (cure deadline: November 17, 2023) to:

(a) Cure the covenant breaches; OR
(b) Submit to Lender a written plan acceptable to Lender demonstrating how Borrower
    intends to restore compliance within a reasonable timeframe.

Failure to cure or present an acceptable plan within the cure period will constitute an
Event of Default under Section 8.1 of the Agreement, entitling Lender to exercise all
remedies available under the Agreement and applicable law, including acceleration of
the outstanding balance of $78,500,000.

The Bank is prepared to discuss potential workout alternatives in good faith. Please
contact James Chen, Senior Vice President, at (503) 555-0147 to arrange a meeting.

This notice is without prejudice to any other rights or remedies of the Lender under
the Agreement or at law.
