Date: November 28, 2023
Loan Number: CRE-2021-00847
Borrower: Cascadia Holdings LLC
Property: Cascadia Tower, Portland, OR
Current Balance: $78,500,000
Risk Rating: 7-DBT (Doubtful)
Workout Officer: Sarah Williams (ANL-0023)

1. SITUATION SUMMARY

Cascadia Tower's occupancy has fallen from 94.2% at origination to 38.0% following the
departure of anchor tenant Apex Technologies and three smaller tenants. The September 2023
reappraisal values the property at $78,000,000, down 40.5% from $131,000,000 at origination.
Four of five loan covenants are in breach.

The borrower, Cascadia Holdings LLC, has been cooperative and is requesting loan modification
rather than pursuing strategic default. David Nakamura (managing member) has contributed
$3.2M of personal funds to cover operating shortfalls since July 2023.

2. PROPOSED RESTRUCTURING

We recommend the following loan modification:

a) PRINCIPAL REDUCTION: Write down from $78,500,000 to $62,000,000 (21.0% haircut)
   - Charge-off of $16,500,000 to be taken in Q4 2023
   - New LTV based on current appraisal: 79.5% ($62M / $78M)

b) MATURITY EXTENSION: Extend from February 2028 to February 2031 (3-year extension)
   - Provides time for market recovery and re-leasing
   
c) RATE REDUCTION: Reduce from 3.875% to 3.25% for 24 months
   - Reduces monthly debt service by approximately $28,000/month
   - Reverts to original rate after 24 months

d) CASH SWEEP: 75% of excess cash flow above operating expenses and debt service
   applied to principal reduction

e) ADDITIONAL COLLATERAL: Borrower to pledge personal guaranty of David Nakamura
   (net worth $24M per financial statement dated October 2023)

f) COVENANT RESET:
   - DSCR minimum: 1.00x for 12 months, then 1.15x, then 1.25x by month 24
   - LTV maximum: 85% for 12 months, then 80%
   - Occupancy minimum: 50% for 12 months, then 65%

3. RECOVERY ANALYSIS

Scenario 1 — Restructure (recommended):
  Expected recovery: $62,000,000 - $68,000,000 (79-87% of current balance)
  Timeline: 3-5 years
  Present value at 6% discount: $52,000,000 - $57,000,000

Scenario 2 — Foreclosure and REO sale:
  Expected recovery: $55,000,000 - $62,000,000 (70-79% of current balance)
  Timeline: 18-30 months (including legal costs of ~$2M)
  Present value at 6% discount: $48,000,000 - $55,000,000

Scenario 3 — Note sale:
  Expected recovery: $54,000,000 - $58,000,000 (69-74% of current balance)
  Indicative bids received from Blackstone ($56M) and Brookfield ($54.5M)
  Timeline: 60-90 days

4. RECOMMENDATION

We recommend Scenario 1 (restructure) based on:
- Highest expected present value recovery
- Cooperative borrower with additional guaranty capacity
- Avoids REO carrying costs and disposition risk
- Portland office market expected to stabilize by 2025-2026

Charge-off of $16,500,000 against ALLL required upon modification approval.
